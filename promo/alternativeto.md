## Description

HelloPlanet is a Windows tray app that turns live Earth and space imagery into automatically refreshed desktop wallpapers. It supports global GOES Earth imagery, Asia-Pacific Himawari 2K/4K satellite tiles, NASA APOD, SDO Sun images, and Mars rover photos.
