# Hacker News Show HN Draft

## Title

Show HN: HelloPlanet - live Earth and space wallpapers for Windows

## Text

I made a small Windows tray app that updates the desktop wallpaper from public Earth and space imagery.

It supports GOES global Earth imagery, Himawari Asia-Pacific 2K/4K satellite tiles, NASA APOD, SDO Sun images, and Mars rover photos. The Himawari part stitches public 550px tiles into larger 2K or 4K wallpapers, which was the most interesting technical bit.

It runs quietly in the system tray, has Chinese/English menus, configurable refresh intervals, and shows the last update time in the tray tooltip.

Repo and exe:
https://gitee.com/yinxiaolong007/hello-planet-earth

I would be curious to hear whether people prefer this kind of small native utility as a tray app, or whether it should eventually become a tiny cross-platform app.
